Eligibility / Qualification

The second-phase Computer Based Test is intended for candidates who qualified in the first phase of the BSF Constable (Tradesmen) recruitment examination.

The first phase consisted of the Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST). Candidates who qualified in these tests are eligible to appear in the written examination as per the applicable recruitment conditions.

The written examination is part of the BSF Constable Tradesmen recruitment process for the 2024-25 recruitment cycle.
