Vacancy Details

The notice gives 27 posts in total. No post is reserved for SC, ST or OBC; candidates of these categories get only the age relaxation admissible in open market recruitment.

A candidate may apply for both Level 1 and Level 2 if eligible, but must submit a separate application form with a separate examination fee for each. Separate examinations will be held for the two levels, tentatively on the same day in two shifts.

Eligibility / Qualification

  • Passed 12th (+2 stage) from a recognized board or its equivalent examination with not less than 50% marks in the aggregate. The 50% marks are not insisted upon for SC / ST / Ex-servicemen for NTPC categories and for candidates with higher qualification such as graduation/post graduation.
  • OR Passed Matriculation from a recognized board plus Course Completed Act Apprenticeship for Technician categories.
  • OR Passed Matriculation from a recognized board plus ITI approved by NCVT/SCVT for technician categories.
  • 10th passed + National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC) granted by NCVT
  • OR 10th pass + ITI
  • OR 10th Pass or ITI or equivalent or National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC) granted by NCVT
  • A President Scouts/Guide/Rover/Ranger OR Himalaya Wood Badge (HWB) holder in any section.
  • Should have been an active member of a Scouts organization for the last 5 years, with the Certificate of Activeness as per Annexure-I (signed by the Group Leader, DOC and DC of Scouts/Guides). This certificate must be issued on or after the date of publication of the notification.
  • Should have attended two events at National level OR All Indian Railway's level and two events at State level.

Candidates should check the official notification for the full eligibility conditions of each post.

Selection Process

Eligible candidates will be called for a written examination (Computer Based Test/Tablet Based Test); those who qualify undergo Document Verification, and the panel is formed on merit of the written examination and the marks on certificates. Candidates recommended for appointment must pass the prescribed medical fitness standards.

  1. The examination is in Hindi or English.
  2. Negative marking: 1/3rd of the marks for the question deducted for every wrong objective answer.
  3. Minimum 40% marks in the written examination are needed for the next stage; relaxation for reserved categories as per Railway Board's rules.
  4. Candidates equal to 5 times the notified vacancies will be called for Document Verification on merit.
  5. Written Examination and Document Verification will be held in Delhi only.

Documents Required

  • Proof of date of birth (10th or Matric pass certificate)
  • Proof of minimum essential educational and scouting qualification prescribed for the post
  • Certificate in prescribed format for SC/ST/OBC/Minority/Economically Backward/PWD candidates (self-attested caste certificate for SC/ST/OBC)
  • Scanned self-attested President Scout/Guide/Rover/Ranger or HWB certificate and parchment
  • Certificate of Activeness issued on or after the date of the notification
  • Photograph in full Scout & Guide uniform (with cap), signature and thumb impression

Other Important Points

  • Bring all uploaded documents in original with one set of self-attested photocopies on the day of Document Verification; certificates not in English or Hindi need a self-attested translation.
  • Serving employees of Central/State Government, Railways or PSUs may apply directly after informing their employer, and must produce a No Objection Certificate on the date of Document Verification.
  • No TA/DA or accommodation is given; candidates bring their own Scout & Guide uniform/kit. SC/ST candidates who uploaded a proper community certificate get free 2nd class travel authority with the E-Admit Card, and must carry the original SC/ST certificate and identity proof while travelling.
  • Shortlisted candidates may need to stay more than one day at the venue at their own arrangement.
  • Applications are rejected for missing photograph, signature or fee, less fee, missing proofs, wrong date of birth, over or under age, or filing in a language other than English or Hindi.
  • Candidates debarred for life from RRB/RRC examinations, or debarred for a period not yet over, need not apply.
  • Candidates with a higher qualification who apply for the lower level are considered for the level applied for.
  • Impersonation, forged certificates or canvassing lead to rejection and debarment; jurisdiction for legal disputes is New Delhi only.
