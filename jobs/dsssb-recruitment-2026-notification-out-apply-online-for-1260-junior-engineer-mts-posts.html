Important: OBC reservation and age relaxation are available only to candidates meeting DSSSB's notified OBC (Delhi) requirements. OBC candidates from outside Delhi are generally considered under the unreserved category for these benefits.

For specified NDMC, MCD, DUSIB and Directorate of Education positions, eligible contractual employees of the same department may receive one-time upper-age relaxation up to the actual period of contractual service, subject to a maximum of five years and the prescribed requirement of at least 180 working days in the relevant year.

This concession does not automatically apply to every applicant or department.

Selection Process

DSSSB will conduct separate combined examinations for Junior Engineer and MTS under their respective post codes.

Post CodeRecruitmentExamination Scheme
809/26Junior Engineer (Electrical)Two-Tier Technical Examination
810/26MTS / General Duty / AayaOne-Tier General Examination

The recruitment also involves online e-dossier submission, document verification and department/post allocation based on merit, eligibility, reservation and preferences. Skill, endurance, driving or trade tests may be conducted where required by the job.

Documents Required

Applicants should keep relevant documents ready for registration, application submission and later e-dossier verification.

  • Class 10 / Matriculation certificate and marksheet.
  • Electrical Engineering degree or diploma, for applicable Junior Engineer positions.
  • Relevant electrical installation, maintenance or professional experience certificates where required.
  • ITI certificate, where applicable to the notified qualification route.
  • Valid photo identity proof.
  • SC/ST certificate, where applicable.
  • OBC (Delhi) Non-Creamy Layer certificate, where applicable.
  • EWS Income and Asset Certificate for the prescribed financial year.
  • PwBD certificate, where applicable.
  • Ex-Servicemen service/discharge documents, where applicable.
  • Meritorious Sportsperson certificate, where applicable.
  • Documents supporting departmental or contractual-service age relaxation.
  • Other certificates required by the concerned department and DSSSB.

Important: The advertisement provides for online e-dossier submission after shortlisting. Candidates must upload supporting documents within the notified e-dossier period. Failure to do so may result in non-consideration of candidature.
