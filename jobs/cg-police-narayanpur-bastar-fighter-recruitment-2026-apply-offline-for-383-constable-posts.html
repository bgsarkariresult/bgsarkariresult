Selection Process

The notified selection procedure includes physical measurement, a Physical Efficiency Test, written examination and interview , followed by the applicable verification and final selection procedures.

  1. Application Scrutiny: Checking the prescribed educational qualification, age, residence and other eligibility conditions.
  2. Physical Measurement: Verification of height, chest measurement for male candidates and prescribed medical standards.
  3. Physical Efficiency Test: Assessment of the notified physical events.
  4. Written Examination: Objective-type examination of general knowledge, mental ability, analytical ability, local geography, culture, traditions, social values and arithmetic.
  5. Interview: Shortlisted candidates will be called for an interview.
  6. Bonus Marks: Awarded to eligible candidates possessing the specifically recognized certificates or qualifications.
  7. Final Selection: Preparation of the merit list under the applicable recruitment and reservation rules.

Exam Pattern & Other Details

Candidates must satisfy the minimum height and chest measurements specified for their category and gender. The notice also requires medical fitness, including acceptable eyesight and the absence of specified physical defects.

  • Candidates must not have knock knees or flat feet.
  • Applicants must possess medically acceptable eyesight.
  • Eye defects and other disqualifying medical conditions will be assessed under the notified recruitment rules.
  • Final determination of physical eligibility will be made by the competent selection or medical authority.
