Selection Process

The recruitment process consists of two Computer Based Tests, followed by document verification and medical examination. Unlike several previous NTPC recruitment cycles, CEN 07/2026 does not require a separate Computer Based Typing Skill Test for Junior Clerk cum Typist or Accounts Clerk cum Typist.

  1. CBT-1: First-stage Computer Based Test for all four posts.
  2. CBT-2: Second-stage Computer Based Test for shortlisted candidates.
  3. Document Verification: Verification of original educational, identity, category and other required documents.
  4. Medical Examination: Candidates must pass the prescribed medical fitness standard for their allotted post.
