According to the official SSC notice dated 23 October 2026, the Computer-Based Examination will be conducted from 2 November to 6 November 2026 for the following papers:

  • General Engineering - Civil and Structural Engineering
  • General Engineering - Electrical Engineering
  • General Engineering - Mechanical Engineering
  • IMD Scientific Assistant - Computer Science and Information Technology
  • IMD Scientific Assistant - Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering
  • IMD Scientific Assistant - Physics

The Commission has specified these six papers for the examination period from 2 to 6 November 2026. Candidates should check their individual examination city information and subsequent admission certificates for their allotted schedule.

  • The Junior Engineer (Telecommunication) examination will be conducted separately from 30 November to 1 December 2026 .
  • The Commission has stated that the examination city information for JE Telecommunication candidates will be made available approximately 8-10 days before their examination .
  • Candidates appearing only for JE Telecommunication should not assume that their examination city slip is available under the 2-6 November examination schedule.

The Staff Selection Commission has announced that the SSC JE Paper-I Admission Certificate will be available tentatively 2-3 days before the respective examination date .

Candidates will be able to download their admission certificates through the same official SSC login portal used to check their examination city.

Other Important Points

  • SSC has issued additional instructions for candidates using the Own Scribe facility.
  • According to the official notice, scribe registrations completed before 5 November 2025 were disposed of. Candidates using the Own Scribe facility may therefore need fresh registration.
  • New scribe registration is integrated with Aadhaar authentication, which is mandatory for the relevant registration process.
  • Candidates opting for their own scribe must provide the scribe's registration number for the generation of the Scribe Entry Pass.
