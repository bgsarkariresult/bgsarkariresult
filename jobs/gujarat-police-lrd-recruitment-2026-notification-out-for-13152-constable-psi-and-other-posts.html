Eligibility / Qualification

The Gujarat Police eligibility requirements differ according to the recruitment cadre. Lokrakshak posts require a Class 12 qualification, PSI Cadre posts require graduation, and Technical Cadre positions require Class 12 together with the prescribed Motor Mechanic Vehicle Trade and apprenticeship qualifications.

Post NameEducational Qualification
Unarmed Police ConstableHigher Secondary School Certificate (Class 12) or equivalent qualification
Armed Police ConstableClass 12 Pass or equivalent qualification
Armed Police Constable (SRPF)Class 12 Pass or equivalent qualification
Jail Sipahi (Male)Class 12 Pass or equivalent qualification
Jail Sipahi (Female/Matron)Class 12 Pass or equivalent qualification
Unarmed Police Sub InspectorBachelor's Degree from a recognized university or equivalent qualification
Jailer Group II (Female)Bachelor's Degree from a recognized university or equivalent qualification
Head Constable Driver Mechanic (Grade II)Class 12 Pass with prescribed Motor Mechanic Vehicle Trade and apprenticeship qualifications
Motor Transport Supervisor (Grade II)Class 12 Pass with prescribed Motor Mechanic Vehicle Trade and apprenticeship qualifications

Candidates applying for Head Constable Driver Mechanic or Motor Transport Supervisor posts must have passed Class 12 or an equivalent examination. In addition, they must possess either of the following prescribed technical qualifications:

  • Motor Mechanic Vehicle Trade Certificate with one year of apprenticeship from the prescribed Government-recognized industrial training institution.
  • Three-year apprenticeship certificate in Motor Mechanic Vehicle Trade under the National Apprenticeship Training Scheme.

A Class 12 qualification alone is not sufficient for Technical Cadre recruitment. Applicants must satisfy the additional trade and apprenticeship requirements specified in their respective advertisements.

  • Basic knowledge of computer applications as prescribed under Gujarat Civil Services recruitment rules.
  • Required knowledge of Gujarati, Hindi or both languages.
  • Compliance with the applicable citizenship and character requirements.
  • Possession of valid educational, reservation and additional qualification certificates by 16 December 2026.
  • Compliance with the prescribed physical and medical fitness standards.

Exam Pattern & Other Details

Candidates must satisfy the minimum physical measurement requirements applicable to their selected cadre. The official notifications prescribe different height requirements for male and female candidates, including concessions for eligible Scheduled Tribe candidates of Gujarat.

The Physical Efficiency Test (PET) for Lokrakshak and PSI cadres is qualifying in nature. Candidates must complete the prescribed running distance within the permitted time to qualify for the subsequent Physical Standards Test.

Candidate CategoryRunning DistanceMaximum Qualifying Time
Male Candidates5,000 Metres25 Minutes
Female Candidates1,600 Metres9 Minutes 30 Seconds
Ex-Servicemen2,400 Metres12 Minutes 30 Seconds

Candidates qualifying in the running test will undergo the prescribed physical measurement examination. Only candidates who qualify in both the Physical Efficiency Test and Physical Standards Test will progress to the written examination for the relevant cadre.

The same running test should not automatically be assumed for Technical Cadre recruitment, which follows a different examination sequence.

The Gujarat Police Lokrakshak written examination consists of 200 objective multiple-choice questions carrying 200 marks. The examination duration is three hours, and candidates must achieve the minimum qualifying marks separately in Part A and Part B.
