Candidates must possess a university degree in Law and have at least three years of standing practice as a Pleader at Bar.

Serving Railway employees with a Law degree may also apply if they have completed at least five years of service in any branch of Railway Administration, subject to the notification's conditions.

Applicants must have a recognized Bachelor's degree in Law and five years of standing as an Advocate at Bar.

Important: A Law degree alone is not sufficient. The prescribed professional practice requirement must also be satisfied.

Candidates need a recognized university degree together with a Diploma in Public Relations, Advertising, Journalism or Mass Communication from a recognized institution or university.

Two years of experience in a relevant field is desirable, not mandatory.

Junior Translator applicants must satisfy one of the prescribed Master's degree language combinations involving Hindi and English, along with an additional translation qualification or experience.

The academic requirement may be fulfilled through a recognized Master's degree in Hindi or English with the other language as a compulsory/elective subject or medium of examination at degree level, or through the alternative Master's degree language combinations specified in Annexure A.

In addition, candidates must possess either:

  • A recognized Diploma or Certificate Course in translation from Hindi to English and vice versa; or
  • Two years of translation experience between Hindi and English in Central or State Government offices, including Government of India undertakings.

Important: A general Master's degree or Hindi/English language proficiency alone does not automatically satisfy this post's eligibility conditions.

Applicants must have graduation along with at least one of the following qualifications:

  • Diploma in Labour or Social Welfare.
  • Diploma in Labour Laws.
  • LLB with papers in Labour Laws.
  • Postgraduate Diploma in Personnel Management.
  • MBA with papers in Personnel Management.
  • MBA in Human Resources.
  • Postgraduate Diploma in Human Resource Management with relevant Personnel Management papers from a Government-recognized institution.

Candidates must possess a Master's degree in Psychology with at least 50% marks in aggregate .

Specialization in Industrial, Organisational or Clinical Psychology; experience in counselling or guidance programmes; and working computer knowledge are desirable qualifications.

Candidates must have passed Class 12 (+2 Stage) or an equivalent examination with Physics and Chemistry as subjects .

This is the only post category in CEN 09/2026 for which the stated essential educational qualification is at the 12th-standard level.

Selection Process

The recruitment involves a single-stage Computer Based Test (CBT) , followed by a Translation Test for Junior Translator candidates, where applicable, and subsequent Document Verification and Medical Examination.

  1. Computer Based Test: Eligible candidates appear for the prescribed objective examination.
  2. Translation Test: Junior Translator (Hindi) candidates shortlisted from CBT must qualify in the translation test.
  3. Document Verification: Candidates are shortlisted according to merit, vacancy availability and applicable reservation rules.
  4. Medical Examination: Candidates must meet the medical standards applicable to their selected post.
  5. Final Empanelment: Subject to merit, qualifications, medical fitness and successful verification.

RRBs reserve the right to conduct additional recruitment stages where necessary under the notification.

Exam Pattern & Other Details

The standard CBT consists of 100 objective questions carrying 100 marks , with a duration of 90 minutes. Eligible candidates using a scribe receive 120 minutes.

Junior Translator (Hindi) candidates will appear for a specialized CBT followed by a qualifying Translation Test.

The CBT is designed to assess knowledge of both Hindi and English. At least 50% of the questions will assess language proficiency, including approximately 20% English and 30% Hindi.

Remaining questions cover General Knowledge, General Intelligence, Simple Arithmetic and basic computer knowledge.
