Selection Process

The selection process consists of an online written examination followed by a personal interview . The final merit calculation gives 70% weightage to Professional Knowledge and 30% to the interview.

  1. Online Written Test: Candidates appear in the objective examination.
  2. Shortlisting: SBI shortlists candidates based on the examination results and applicable requirements.
  3. Personal Interview: Shortlisted candidates attend an interview carrying 100 marks.
  4. Document Verification: Educational qualifications, service experience, medical fitness and category certificates are verified.
  5. Final Merit List: Prepared using normalized Professional Knowledge and interview marks.

SBI reserves the right to modify the selection process, including considering shortlisting followed by interview, if required.
