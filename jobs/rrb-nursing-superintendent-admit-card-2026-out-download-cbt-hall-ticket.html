Selection Process

The selection process under CEN 05/2026 includes:

  1. Computer-Based Test (CBT)
  2. Document Verification
  3. Medical Examination

Final appointment is subject to performance in the applicable recruitment stages and fulfilment of the required educational, professional and medical eligibility conditions.

Documents Required

Candidates must follow the document requirements printed on their individual e-call letters.

  • Printed RRB Nursing Superintendent Admit Card 2026
  • Original Aadhaar Card or a printout of e-verified Aadhaar for biometric verification
  • Additional prescribed identity proof, if required by the e-call letter
  • Photograph or any other document specifically mentioned in the examination instructions

The RRB examination schedule states that Aadhaar-linked biometric authentication will be conducted at the examination centre before entry into the examination hall.
