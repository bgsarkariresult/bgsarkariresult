Selection Process

The selection procedure includes an online objective examination and a Local Language Proficiency Test (LLPT) . Indian Bank may also conduct an interview at its discretion.

  1. NATS Registration: Eligible candidates complete their apprenticeship registration and profile.
  2. Online Application: Candidates submit the application and pay the applicable fee.
  3. Online Written Test: Candidates appear in the 100-mark objective examination.
  4. Local Language Proficiency Test: Qualified candidates must demonstrate proficiency in the selected State/UT language unless exempt.
  5. Interview, If Conducted: The bank may include or dispense with an interview.
  6. Merit List: Candidates are ranked according to the applicable State/UT and category-wise merit rules.
  7. Document Verification: Eligibility and supporting certificates are verified.
  8. Medical Fitness: Engagement is subject to the bank's prescribed medical fitness requirements.
  9. Apprenticeship Contract: Selected candidates receive their digital engagement through NATS 2.0.

Other Important Points

Selected candidates will receive a digital apprenticeship engagement offer through NATS 2.0. The apprenticeship contract is normally for 12 months and terminates automatically after completion of the prescribed training period.

Candidates must report at the allotted bank branch or training venue on the specified joining date. Failure to report may lead to termination of the apprenticeship contract.
