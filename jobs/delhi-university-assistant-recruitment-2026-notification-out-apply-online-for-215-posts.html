Eligibility / Qualification

Applicants must possess a Bachelor's degree from a recognized institution or university , have two years of qualifying work experience and meet the prescribed typing and computer requirements.

RequirementEligibility
Educational QualificationBachelor's Degree from a Recognized Institute or University
Experience2 Years as Junior Assistant or in an Equivalent Post
English Typing35 Words Per Minute
Hindi Typing30 Words Per Minute
Computer KnowledgeProficiency in Computer Operations

Candidates need to satisfy either the English or Hindi typing speed requirement, not both.

The required two-year experience must be as a Junior Assistant or in an equivalent position in one of the following organizations:

  • Universities or research establishments.
  • Central or State Government organizations.
  • Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs).
  • Autonomous bodies.
  • Reputed private companies or corporate banks offering an equivalent pay package, with minimum annual turnover of Rs. 200 crore.

Important: Candidates claiming experience in a private company or corporate bank must satisfy both the equivalent-pay and minimum-turnover conditions stated in the advertisement.

The prescribed qualification and experience must be possessed by the applicable application closing date.

Age Limit

The maximum age limit for Assistant posts is 32 years . The age will be calculated as on the closing date of the advertisement, with relaxation for eligible categories.

Age CriteriaDetails
Maximum Age32 Years
Age Calculation DateApplication Closing Date
Age RelaxationAs per Central Government Orders Adopted by the University

Age relaxation is available to eligible SC, ST, OBC, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen and other categories under applicable Central Government orders adopted by Delhi University.

Applicants with at least three years of regular service in the same or allied field in eligible government, university, autonomous or PSU organizations may receive relaxation up to five years or the number of completed years of qualifying service, whichever is less.

The notification also provides specific age exemptions for eligible departmental candidates and certain contractual, daily-wage or ad hoc employees directly appointed by Delhi University or its affiliated/constituent colleges.

Important: The special contractual-employee exemption does not apply to outsourced employees or project-based contractual assignments.

Selection Process

The selection process consists of a Preliminary Test, Main Examination and qualifying Skill Test . The Preliminary Test is used for shortlisting, while the Main Examination determines merit, subject to qualification in the Skill Test.

  1. Preliminary Test - Objective MCQ examination.
  2. Main Examination - Descriptive examination.
  3. Skill Test - Computer proficiency, office procedures, noting, drafting and typing.
  4. Document Verification - Verification of qualification, experience and other eligibility claims.
  5. Final Merit - Based on Main Examination marks, subject to applicable selection rules.
