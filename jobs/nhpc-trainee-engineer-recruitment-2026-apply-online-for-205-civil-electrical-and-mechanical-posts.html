The NHPC Trainee Engineer Recruitment 2026 eligibility conditions are based on educational qualifications, valid GATE examination scores, age and reservation requirements. Candidates must possess a full-time Engineering Degree in the prescribed discipline or an eligible AMIE qualification. Only the notified Engineering disciplines and corresponding GATE paper codes are accepted.

NHPC has prescribed a full-time regular Bachelor's Degree in Engineering, Technology or B.Sc Engineering in the relevant discipline from a recognised Indian university or AICTE-approved institution. An eligible AMIE qualification with enrolment up to 31 May 2013 is also accepted. Candidates must have secured at least 60% marks, relaxed to 50% for eligible SC, ST and PwBD candidates.

Selection Process

Candidates must produce the required educational, category, GATE and other supporting documents during the selection process. Final empanelment will depend on the prescribed composite merit, minimum qualifying standards, document verification and other eligibility conditions.

NHPC has prescribed a combined selection weightage of 75% for GATE 2025 or GATE 2026 normalised marks, 5% for Group Discussion and 20% for Personal Interview. The final score will be calculated out of 100 using these components.

Exam Pattern & Other Details

The NHPC Trainee Engineer Recruitment 2026 does not prescribe a separate NHPC written examination. Selection is based on valid GATE 2025 or GATE 2026 scores in Civil, Electrical or Mechanical Engineering, followed by Group Discussion and Personal Interview. Candidates must have qualified in the corresponding GATE paper before applying.
