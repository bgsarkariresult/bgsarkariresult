Selection Process

The selection process varies according to the discipline. The official notification specifies separate procedures for Legal posts and other executive positions.

Selection for Deputy Manager (Legal) will be based on a Computer Based Test (CBT) followed by a Personal Interview.

  1. Computer Based Test: 80 Marks
  2. Personal Interview: 20 Marks
  3. Minimum Qualifying Marks in CBT: 50%
  4. Interview shortlisting ratio: 1:6, subject to the notification
  5. Final merit based on combined CBT and interview marks

For disciplines other than Legal, selection will be based on a Personal Interview. If the number of eligible applicants exceeds the prescribed shortlisting ratio, NLC India Limited may conduct a Screening Test through CBT before the interview.

The minimum qualifying marks in the Personal Interview are 50% for UR / EWS candidates and 40% for SC / ST / OBC (NCL) / PwBD candidates against applicable reserved posts. Candidates must also satisfy document verification and medical fitness requirements.
