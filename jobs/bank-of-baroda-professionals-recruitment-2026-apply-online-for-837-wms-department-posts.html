Selection Process

Selection may include application scrutiny, online examination, psychometric assessment, other suitable tests and personal interview , as decided by Bank of Baroda.

  1. Application Scrutiny: Applications are reviewed against the relevant qualifications, experience, certifications and other criteria.
  2. Online Test, If Conducted: Candidates may undergo an online examination if the Bank includes it in the selection process.
  3. Psychometric Assessment: The Bank may assess personality, communication, problem-solving and suitability.
  4. Personal Interview: Shortlisted applicants may be called for an interview.
  5. Document Verification: Original certificates and experience documents will be checked.
  6. Final Selection: Candidates will be selected according to the applicable assessment results, eligibility and vacancies.

Important: The notification does not confirm that every candidate will appear for every assessment stage. The Bank may change or modify its selection procedure according to recruitment requirements.

Documents Required

Candidates must upload documents supporting their educational qualification, professional certification, experience, identity and applicable reservation claims.

  • Current resume or bio-data.
  • Class 10 certificate or other accepted date-of-birth proof.
  • Graduation degree certificate and marksheets.
  • Postgraduate management qualification certificate, where claimed.
  • Relevant NISM, IRDA, CFP or CFA certificates.
  • Employment and experience certificates.
  • Appointment letters and relieving letters, where applicable.
  • Latest salary slips and documents showing CTC breakup.
  • Valid photo identity proof.
  • SC/ST/OBC/EWS certificates, where applicable.
  • PwBD certificate, where applicable.
  • Ex-Servicemen service or discharge documents, where applicable.
  • No Objection Certificate for applicable currently employed candidates at the required verification stage.
  • Recent passport-size photograph.
  • Scanned signature.
  • Other certificates requested by the Bank during application or selection.

Original eligibility documents and self-attested copies must be produced when called for interview or document verification. Failure to produce the relevant certificates may result in rejection.
