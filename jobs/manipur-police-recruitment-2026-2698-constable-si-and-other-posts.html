Eligibility / Qualification

The recruitment is open to willing and eligible candidates for the notified posts. However, the uploaded notification does not provide detailed post-wise educational qualifications, age limits or physical measurement standards.

Applicants should obtain the prescribed recruitment instructions and confirm the specific eligibility requirements for their chosen post from the concerned District SP office or official Manipur Police website.

Age Limit

Candidates whose applications were previously submitted and duly sponsored by the concerned Employment Exchange under the specified earlier notifications do not need to apply afresh. Such candidates will also receive a one-time relaxation in the upper age limit.

This provision applies to eligible applicants under the earlier recruitment notifications dated 14 July 2021, 17 October 2022 and 13 March 2024, as identified in the new notification.

Application Fee

Manipur Police has prescribed a fee of Rs. 400 per application form . Eligible Persons with Disabilities candidates are exempted from paying the application fee.
