Selection Process

The selection process consists of a qualifying Preliminary CBT and a merit-based Mains CBT, followed by eligibility and document verification by ICAR.

  1. Online application submission.
  2. Combined Preliminary Computer Based Test.
  3. Shortlisting for Mains in a 1:15 ratio against functional-group vacancies, subject to qualifying marks.
  4. Combined Mains Computer Based Test.
  5. Preparation of functional group/subgroup-wise merit lists.
  6. Post allocation according to rank, eligibility, preferences and available vacancies.
  7. Verification of original qualifications and other relevant documents by ICAR.
  8. Appointment subject to applicable conditions and suitability.

No Interview: The notification explicitly confirms that no interview will be conducted. Final selection merit is based on Mains Examination performance only.
