Selection Process

  1. Screening and scrutiny of applications
  2. Merit list from average of 10th and ITI percentage marks with equal weightage
  3. Document Verification with medical certificate
  4. No written test or viva
  5. Merit from average of 10th and ITI percentage marks with equal weightage
  6. Tie: older candidate first, then the one who passed matriculation earlier
  7. Final merit list prepared cluster-wise, trade-wise and community-wise equal to number of slots
  8. Mismatch between marks entered online and original mark sheet at Document Verification leads to rejection

Documents Required

  • Proof of date of birth (10th certificate or DOB certificate)
  • 10th pass mark sheet and certificate
  • Consolidated ITI mark sheet of all semesters / Provisional National Trade Certificate with marks
  • National Trade Certificate issued by NCVT/SCVT with mark sheet
  • SC/ST/OBC/PWD/Ex-SM certificate in prescribed format (annexure on www.rrcnr.org)
  • Discharge or serving certificate for Ex-servicemen quota applicants

Other Important Points

  • Must meet physical fitness standards under the Apprentices Act 1961 and Apprenticeship Rules 1992
  • PwBD candidates must apply against slots notified for their type of disability
  • Application must be filled in English or Hindi
  • Only one application per candidate; multiple applications are all rejected
  • Must apply to a cluster and unit where slots for own ITI trade are notified
  • Merit list expected to be displayed in February 2027
  • Training slots are provisional and may increase or decrease
  • No change allowed in the application after final submission
  • No TA/DA or accommodation for appearing in the process
  • Document Verification place and time will be given on the E-Admit Card on the RRC website
  • No request to change division or unit will be entertained
