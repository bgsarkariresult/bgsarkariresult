How to Apply Online for RRB Station Master Recruitment 2026?
Visit the official website rrbapply.gov.in .
Open the RRB recruitment application portal.
Select the Station Master recruitment under CEN 08/2026 .
Click on Create an Account for new registration or Already Have an Account to log in.
Complete the required account verification and enter personal details.
Fill in educational qualifications and applicable category details.
Select the Railway Recruitment Board and available Railway preferences carefully.
Capture the required live photograph and upload the signature and applicable documents according to the portal instructions.
Review all entered details and submit the application form.
Pay the applicable examination fee through the online payment facility.
