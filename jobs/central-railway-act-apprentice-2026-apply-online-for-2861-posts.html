Reservation applies to eligible SC, ST, OBC, PwBD and Ex-Servicemen categories according to the relevant notification provisions.

The advertisement provides reservation provisions for eligible Ex-Servicemen and certain children of Ex-Servicemen or serving Armed Forces personnel.

Applicants claiming these benefits must upload the required discharge or serving certificate and produce the original supporting documents when called for verification.

Eligibility / Qualification

Candidates must have passed Class 10 with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks from a recognized board and hold an appropriate ITI trade certificate issued or provisionally certified by NCVT/SCVT.

Important Qualification Restriction: Engineering graduates and diploma holders are not eligible for apprenticeship under this specific notification, as they are covered by a separate apprenticeship scheme. Applicants must satisfy the exact Class 10 and ITI eligibility requirements stated by RRC Central Railway.

PwBD eligibility is restricted to the disability categories and trade-specific fitness conditions stated in the notification. The applicable provisions include certain locomotor disabilities, cerebral palsy, hearing disabilities and low-vision categories.

Candidates claiming PwBD reservation must possess a valid disability certificate from the competent medical authority. Not every disability category is suitable for every apprenticeship trade, and applicants must check the requirements applicable to their selected trade.

Selection Process

Selection will be based on a merit list calculated using the simple average of Class 10 and ITI percentage marks . The notification does not prescribe a separate written examination or interview.

  1. Online Application: Candidates apply through the RRC Central Railway website.
  2. Merit Calculation: Class 10 aggregate percentage and ITI percentage are given equal weightage.
  3. Merit List Preparation: Lists are prepared cluster-wise, unit-wise, trade-wise and community-wise.
  4. Document Verification: Shortlisted candidates must produce original educational and category certificates.
  5. Medical Fitness: Candidates must present the prescribed medical fitness certificate.
  6. Apprenticeship Engagement: Eligible candidates are engaged for training after completing the necessary formalities.

Important: Although RRC Central Railway has issued a centralized notification, it will not prepare one centralized merit list for all 2,861 slots. Selection is organized according to the relevant cluster, unit, trade and community.

Documents Required

Applicants must upload legible scanned copies of the required educational and supporting documents during the online application process.

  • Class 10 or equivalent marksheet.
  • Date-of-birth proof through Matriculation certificate, marksheet or accepted school leaving certificate.
  • Consolidated ITI marksheet covering all semesters of the notified trade.
  • National Trade Certificate issued by NCVT or accepted provisional certificate issued by NCVT/SCVT.
  • SC/ST/OBC caste certificate, where applicable.
  • Valid OBC Non-Creamy Layer certificate, where applicable.
  • Disability certificate for eligible PwBD applicants.
  • Discharge certificate or Armed Forces serving certificate for applicable Ex-Servicemen reservation claims.
  • Prescribed recent colour photograph.
  • Scanned candidate signature.
  • Aadhaar or other permitted identity document details.

Original documents must be produced during document verification. Documents issued in languages other than Hindi or English must be accompanied by the required attested translation.

Candidates shortlisted through the merit process will be called for document verification in the relevant division or unit according to their selected cluster.

They must produce the original educational certificates, ITI marksheets, reservation certificates, identity documents and other applicable records.
