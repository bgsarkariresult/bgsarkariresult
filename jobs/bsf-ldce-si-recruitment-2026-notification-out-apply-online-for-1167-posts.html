Selection Process

  1. Stage-I: Written Examination (CBT Mode)
  2. Stage-II: Physical Efficiency Test (PET), Documents Verification and Medical Examination
  3. CBT: one objective paper of 200 marks in 03:30 hrs, four subjects of 50 marks each
  4. PET is qualifying only; failure in one event leads to disqualification
  5. Detailed Medical Examination by Medical Boards of concerned CAPFs, with Review Medical Examination
  6. Tie in CBT resolved by date of birth (older higher), then alphabetical order of first name
  7. Final merit list prepared by Nodal Force; selected list uploaded on BSF website

Documents Required

  • Service cum eligibility certificate (Annexure-III)
  • Undertaking (Annexure-IV)
  • NOC from competent authority (Annexure-V)
  • Photograph not more than 3 months old, without cap and spectacles
  • Signature and thumb impression
  • Originals of age proof, educational qualification, caste certificate and Medical Fitness Certificate at DV

  • Objection link on BSF website 2-3 days after CBT
  • No re-evaluation or rechecking of scores

Other Important Points

  • Four years regular service including basic training
  • Minimum Good APAR grading of last five years (four years if only 4 years of service)
  • Unblemished clean record of entire service until issue of offer of appointment
  • Medical category SHAPE-I; Low Medical Category applications not entertained
  • Physical standards: male general height 170 cm, chest 80/85 cm; female general height 157 cm
  • A total of 03 chances to appear in the LDC Exam
  • No change of exam centre will be considered
  • No correction allowed after final submission
  • No TA/DA for attending LDCE; period treated as leave
  • Candidates on deputation/attachment apply through parent units for NOC and certificates
  • Vacancies may increase or decrease due to administrative reasons
