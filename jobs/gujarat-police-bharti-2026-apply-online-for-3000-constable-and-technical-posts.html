Eligibility / Qualification

Candidates applying for Gujarat Police Recruitment 2026 must satisfy the prescribed educational qualifications and other eligibility conditions for their respective posts. The official newspaper advertisement specifies the educational qualifications for the Lokrakshak and Motor Transport Technical cadres.

For the Lokrakshak cadre, candidates must have passed the Higher Secondary School Certificate (HSC) Examination from the Secondary and Higher Secondary Education Board or possess an equivalent qualification recognised by the Government.

This qualification applies to the Unarmed Police Constable, Armed Police Constable and Armed Police Constable (SRPF) posts advertised under GPRB/202627/1.

Candidates applying for the Head Constable Driver Mechanic Grade-2 post must satisfy both the general educational qualification and the prescribed technical training requirement.

  • Passed the Higher Secondary School Certificate (Class 12) examination or an equivalent qualification recognised by the Government.
  • Possess a Motor Mechanic Vehicle Trade Certificate along with one year of apprenticeship from a government-recognised industrial institute; or
  • Possess a three-year apprenticeship certificate in the Motor Mechanic Vehicle Trade under the National Apprenticeship Training Scheme.

For the Motor Transport Supervisor Grade-2 post, candidates must meet the following educational and technical requirements:

  • Passed the Higher Secondary School Certificate (Class 12) examination or an equivalent qualification recognised by the Government.
  • Possess a Motor Mechanic Vehicle Trade Certificate along with one year of apprenticeship from a government-recognised Industrial Training Institute (ITI); or
  • Possess a three-year apprenticeship certificate in the Motor Mechanic Vehicle Trade under the National Apprenticeship Training Scheme.

The detailed recruitment advertisements, scheduled for publication on 26 November 2026, should be consulted for all additional eligibility requirements, certificate conditions and applicable recruitment rules.
