Selection Process

The recruitment involves multi-stage evaluation based on qualifications, research output, and performance before the Selection Committee:

  1. Screening and Shortlisting: Meeting essential criteria does not guarantee a call. The Screening Committee reserves the right to set higher shortlisting criteria, including micro-specialization, superior academic record (higher grades in UG/PG), NIRF or QS rankings of degree-granting institutions/CFTIs/State Government universities, number of SCI/SCIE/SSCI indexed journal publications with Impact Factor, utility patents, and role/value of sponsored research projects. The institute also reserves the right to restrict the number of candidates for interviews to a reasonable limit based on qualifications, experience, credit points, etc.
  2. Written Test and/or Presentation/Demo: Shortlisted candidates may be required to appear for a written examination, teaching demonstration, or presentation. No request for change of date of Written Test and/or Presentation/Demo/Interview will be entertained under any circumstances.
  3. Personal Interview: Interview before the duly constituted Selection Committee. No travel allowance (TA) will be paid for attending the test or interview.
  4. Final Approval: Recommendations of the Selection Committee are subject to final approval and appointment by the Board of Governors (BoG).

Documents Required

  • Downloaded and signed printout of the online application form.
  • Valid Photo ID proof (Passport, Voter ID, PAN Card, Aadhar Card, or Government-issued ID).
  • Mark sheets and degree certificates for Bachelor's, Master's, and Ph.D. degrees.
  • Certificate issued by the authorized signatory (not Mentor/Supervisor/Head of the Department) of the Institute/University certifying first class, or equivalent conversion on a 10-point scale, if CGPA was awarded on a scale other than a 10-point scale.
  • Experience certificates, offer letters, appointment letters, and latest salary slips or pay-fixation orders.
  • Copies of research publications in SCI/SCIE/SSCI indexed journals, patent grant documents, and sponsored research project sanction letters.
  • Valid Category Certificate for SC/ST/OBC (Non-Creamy Layer)/EWS candidates issued by an authority not below the rank of Tehsildar or First-Class Magistrate (OBC-NCL and EWS certificates must be obtained on or after 01.04.2026).
  • Disability Certificate for PwBD candidates.
  • No Objection Certificate (NOC) and Vigilance Clearance Certificate (VCC) from the employer in the prescribed format (Annexure-I) for candidates serving in Government, Semi-Government, PSUs, or Universities (or undertaking in lieu thereof).

Other Important Points

  • For Engineering: B.E./B.Tech. or equivalent and M.E./M.Tech. or equivalent degree in the relevant discipline. Candidates possessing a Ph.D. directly after B.Tech./B.E. from reputed institutions are also considered provided they have First Class at the Bachelor's level.
  • For Mathematics: First Class at both UG and PG levels (60% marks or 6.5/10 CGPA) with Ph.D. in the relevant discipline.
  • Countable Experience: Only experience rendered on a regular basis in applicable pay scales as per UGC, AICTE, Government of India, or PSUs is counted. Experience rendered on part-time, ad-hoc, daily wage, visiting, guest faculty, fixed remuneration, contractual, or temporary basis will not be considered.
  • Equal Treatment: There shall be no distinction between external and internal candidates with regard to the requirements of qualification and experience.
  • Age Limit: Fresh appointment beyond the age of 60 years is discouraged except in the case of faculty with an exceptionally brilliant research career and with an ongoing or approved externally funded research project. The crucial date for determining eligibility and preferred age limit is 21 November 2026.

Candidates need to go through the Recruitment Rules for Faculty Positions at IIIT Sonepat (as per F.No.27-11/2011 TS.1 dated 23 April 2014) along with the advertisement before starting to fill the application form.

  • Incomplete, unbound, or loosely compiled hard copy applications, as well as applications received after 15 December 2026 (5:00 PM), will be summarily rejected without considering postal delays.

  • Candidates must bring original documents and a valid photo ID proof to the written test, presentation, or interview; failure to present them will lead to debarment.

  • Selected candidates must be found medically fit through a medical examination conducted by the designated medical authority.
  • The Institute reserves the right to modify, defer, or cancel full or part of the recruitment at any stage without assigning any reason, and reserves the right not to fill any post(s).
  • In case of any inadvertent error detected at any stage even after issuing an appointment letter, the institute reserves the right to modify, withdraw, or cancel any communication made to candidates.
  • No correspondence will be entertained regarding postal delays, conduct and result of interview, or reasons for not being called; canvassing in any form leads to disqualification.
  • Any subsequent corrigendum, changes, or updates will be published only on the institute website (www.iiitsonepat.ac.in). Only selected candidates will be informed through email.
