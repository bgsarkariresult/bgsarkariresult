Applicants must possess a recognized Bachelor's degree in the prescribed engineering branch and must have qualified in the corresponding GATE 2025 paper.

Graduate Executive Trainees will be inducted in E-2 Grade with a basic pay of Rs. 50,000 per month during their one-year training period. The approximate annual CTC is Rs. 14.50 lakh .

Shortlisted candidates will be called for document verification before the Personal Interview. They must produce original qualification certificates, GATE 2025 scorecard, category certificates and other applicable records.

Candidates employed in Government, Central/State PSUs or qualifying organizations must submit the required No Objection Certificate.

Finally selected candidates must undergo a pre-employment medical examination and be declared medically fit according to NLCIL standards before appointment.

  • Recruitment is based on GATE 2025 scores.
  • Only Mechanical, Electrical, Civil and Mining disciplines are notified.
  • Online registration opens on 13 November 2026.
  • The last date for registration is 14 December 2026.
  • The fee payment deadline is 14 December 2026 at 11:45 PM.

  • Qualifications and reservation eligibility are assessed as on 1 November 2026.
  • Minimum aggregate marks are 60% for UR/EWS/OBC-NCL and 50% for SC/ST, subject to the applicable vacancy conditions.
