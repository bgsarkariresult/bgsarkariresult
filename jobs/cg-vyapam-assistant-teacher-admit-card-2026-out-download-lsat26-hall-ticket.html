Important Dates

The Assistant Teacher Written Recruitment Test (LSAT26) was initially scheduled for 11 October 2026. However, the examination was postponed following an order of the High Court of Chhattisgarh, Bilaspur.

Subsequent examination updates have 6 December 2026 as the revised examination date. Candidates should confirm the applicable schedule through the official CG Vyapam notices and their latest admit cards.
