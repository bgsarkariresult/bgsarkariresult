Eligibility / Qualification

To meet the CWC eligibility, a candidate must satisfy the nationality, qualification and age conditions as on the last date of application, 15.11.2026.

  • A candidate must be a citizen of India, a subject of Nepal or Bhutan, a Tibetan refugee who came to India before 1 January 1962 with the intention of settling permanently, or a person of Indian origin who has migrated from Pakistan, Burma, Sri Lanka, East African countries of Kenya, Uganda, the United Republic of Tanzania, Zambia, Malawi, Zaire, Ethiopia or Vietnam with the intention of settling permanently in India. Candidates in the last four groups must hold a certificate of eligibility issued by the Government of India.
  • The minimum age to apply for all the posts is 18 years.
  • All qualifications must be from a University/Institution/Board recognized by the Government of India or approved by Government regulatory bodies. Candidates appearing in the final year or final semester are not eligible.
  • Where SGPA/CGPA/OGPA is awarded, it should be converted into equivalent percentage in the online application.
  • No change of category is permitted after registration.

  • With cumulative age relaxation, the maximum age limit cannot exceed 55 years as on 15.11.2026.
  • For Ex-Servicemen (post codes 07 and 08), the period of actual military service is deducted from the actual age, and the resultant age should not exceed the maximum age limit by more than 3 years (8 years for SC/ST and 6 years for OBC candidates).

  • Age concession is not admissible to sons, daughters and dependents of Ex-Servicemen. SC/ST/OBC/EWS candidates applying against unreserved posts get no age or qualifying-marks relaxation.

Application Fee

The CWC application fee is Rs. 850 plus intimation charges of Rs. 500 (Rs. 1,350 in total) for UR, EWS and OBC candidates. SC, ST, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen and women candidates are exempt from the application fee but must pay intimation charges of Rs. 500. The amount is inclusive of GST, and the payment gateway charges are borne by the candidate.

CategoryApplication FeeIntimation ChargesTotal
SC, ST, PwBD, Ex-Serviceman and women candidatesNilRs. 500Rs. 500/-
Other candidates (UR/EWS/OBC)Rs. 850Rs. 500Rs. 1,350/-
  • The fee is non-refundable and is paid through the online payment gateway only. Payment by DD, cheque or postal order is not accepted.
  • Payment can be made by debit card (RuPay/Visa/MasterCard/Maestro), credit card, internet banking, UPI, IMPS, cash cards or mobile wallets.
  • A candidate applying for more than one post must apply and pay the fee separately for each post.
  • On successful payment, an e-receipt is generated. If no e-receipt is generated, the payment has failed, and the candidate should log in again with the Provisional Registration Number and Password and repeat the payment.

Selection Process

Post CodeSelection Process
01, 02, 03, 04, 05 and 06 (Assistant Manager posts)Online Test + Document Verification followed by Interview
07 and 08 (Junior Executive posts)Online Test + Document Verification
  1. Candidates are shortlisted from the online test in the ratio of 1:5 for the Assistant Manager posts (Interview) and 1:3 for the Junior Executive posts (Document Verification). The Interview and Document Verification are conducted at Delhi.
  2. For the Assistant Manager posts, the Interview carries 30 marks. The minimum qualifying marks in the Interview are 50% for Unreserved and EWS candidates, 45% for OBC and 40% for SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen candidates. The weightage of the online exam and the Interview is 85:15.
  3. For the Junior Executive posts, appointment is based on the marks obtained in the online test, after successful Document Verification.
  4. Each candidate must obtain a minimum score in each section and a minimum total score. The minimum qualifying marks for the Professional/Subject Knowledge test (posts 02 to 06 and 08) and for the Language & Comprehension Abilities Descriptive Test (all posts) are 40% for Unreserved and 35% for OBC/EWS/SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen candidates.
  5. If two or more candidates get the same score, the senior candidate by date of birth is placed higher. A wait list up to 100% of the vacancies in each category may be kept.
  6. Cut-offs are applied in two stages, on the scores in individual tests and on the total score. If the exam is held in more than one session, the scores are equated and normalized.

Exam Pattern & Other Details

The online examination is of 3 hours in total, with each test separately timed. There is a penalty of one-fourth of the marks of the question for each wrong answer, and there is no penalty for an unanswered question. Each post has 202 questions and 230 maximum marks.

Other Important Points

  • All details in the online application, including name, category, date of birth, post, address, mobile number, e-mail ID and exam centre, are final, and no change is allowed after submission.
  • Online applications cannot be withdrawn, and the fee is neither refunded nor held in reserve for any other examination. For multiple applications for the same post, only the latest valid application is retained and the fee for the others is forfeited.
  • Candidature at every stage is provisional. CWC can cancel the candidature at any stage if the candidate does not meet the eligibility norms or has given false information.
  • Candidates must carry the call letter, the original photo identity proof and its photocopy to the exam. The name on the call letter must match the photo identity proof. Ration cards and learner's driving licences are not accepted as ID proof.
  • Biometric data (left thumb impression) and a photograph are captured on the day of the exam and verified at Document Verification/Interview.
  • Candidates reporting late are not permitted to take the exam. Candidates may need to be at the venue for up to 4 hours or more.
  • Items not allowed in the exam centre include mobile phones, Bluetooth devices, calculators, watches, handbags, belts, caps, metallic items and eatables. Only half-sleeved clothing and open-toe footwear are allowed.
  • Use of unfair means can lead to disqualification, debarment and an FIR. CWC analyses the answers of candidates to detect similarity patterns.
  • Candidates with disabilities using a scribe get compensatory time of 20 minutes per hour or as advised. The scribe must be one step lower in qualification and must not be a candidate for any post in this advertisement.
  • Intimations are sent by e-mail and SMS only. All announcements and any corrigendum are published only on www.cewacor.nic.in. In case of a dispute over interpretation, the English version prevails.
  • Appointment is subject to medical fitness. No person dismissed or compulsorily retired from CWC, a State or Central Government department or a PSU, or convicted for an offence involving moral turpitude, is eligible.
